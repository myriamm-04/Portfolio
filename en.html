What is BTS SIO?

The BTS SIO (Services Informatiques aux Organisations) is a 2-year higher education program that trains IT professionals capable of designing, developing, and maintaining software solutions within companies. The SLAM option (Software Solutions and Business Applications) focuses mainly on application development (web, mobile, or desktop software), programming, database management, and maintaining existing applications. Students learn to analyze user needs, design custom software solutions, and manage the different phases of an application's lifecycle. This training also prepares us to work in project-based environments while meeting quality, security, and deadline constraints, all while developing professional skills through mandatory internships.

Technology Watch

Topic 1: AI and Automation in Software Development

Today, artificial intelligence is revolutionizing the way we code. With assistants like GitHub Copilot or ChatGPT, we can generate code, fix bugs, or automate tests much faster. This boosts productivity, but it also raises major questions regarding code security, ethics, and the reliability of what is being generated.

Topic 2: Quantum Computing

Unlike our current computers that operate with bits (0 or 1), quantum computing uses qubits. Thanks to the phenomenon of superposition, a qubit can be a 0, a 1, or both at the same time. This allows complex calculations to be executed in parallel at an outstanding speed.
